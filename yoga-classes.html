Find Your Practice, Restore Your Flow

At Echo Yoga Space, we believe that yoga is not about flexibility or perfect poses, but about self-discovery and connection. Our classes cater to all levels of experience, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners seeking to deepen their path.

We offer a variety of styles designed to match your energy level and intentions for the day. Whether you want to build strength and sweat in an active flow, or slow down and turn inward with passive stretches, we have a space for you.

Our Class Varieties

Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic practice linking breath with movement. Build strength, endurance, and mindfulness as you flow from one posture to the next.

Gentle Hatha

A slower-paced class focusing on foundational postures, alignment, and breathing techniques. Perfect for beginners and grounding.

Yin & Restore

A deeply relaxing practice using props to support the body in passive postures held for longer periods, targeting deep connective tissues.

Chakra Flow

Classes tailored to specific energy centers, combining poses, breath work, and visualization to release blocks and restore balance.
