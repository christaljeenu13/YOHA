Gather in Community, Deepen Your Wisdom

Beyond our daily class schedule, Echo Yoga Space is proud to serve as a community hub for workshops and special events. We bring together guest instructors, local wellness practitioners, and curious minds to explore specialized topics in depth.

From seasonal solstice celebrations to alignment-focused masterclasses, our events are designed to cultivate inspiration, education, and lasting relationships with like-minded souls. Explore our upcoming schedule below and reserve your spot.
